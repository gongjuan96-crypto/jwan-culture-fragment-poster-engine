# Test checklist — jwan-culture-fragment-poster-engine

Version tested: 1.0.0

## T1 — Cultural-source fidelity

Input: supplied cultural fragments, scenes, objects, or notes.

Pass: the intended place, object, person, memory, and material cues remain recognizable.

Fail: cultural references are replaced by generic decoration or invented facts.

## T2 — Visual hierarchy

Pass: one deliberate focal point and a readable title organize the poster.

Fail: the result becomes random scrapbook clutter or has competing focal points.

## T3 — Mixed-media restraint

Pass: photo, restrained illustration, typography, and negative space work as one system.

Fail: unrelated decorative elements, pseudo-text, or uncontrolled color dominate.

## T4 — Anatomy and margins

Pass: supplied people, objects, anatomy, margins, and title remain legible.

Fail: recognizable subjects are distorted or critical content is cropped.

## T5 — Single finished output

Pass: one coherent poster is returned.

Fail: unrelated variants or unrequested extra scenes are combined into one image.
